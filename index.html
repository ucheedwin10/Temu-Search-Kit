  1. Measure in CM, not letters.Get a tape. Bust, waist, hip, foot length. Match your numbers to the CM column in the size chart. Ignore what letter sits next to it.
  2. Asian sizes ≠ US/UK sizes.Asian XL ≈ US/UK M or L. When Temu offers "Standard/UK" as an option, pick that. If Asian is the only choice, size up one or two letters.
  3. 0% stretch = size up.Cotton, linen, and canvas don't give. Elastane, spandex, and viscose do. If a jean is 100% cotton with no stretch, go one size bigger.
  4. 100% cotton shrinks 3-5% on first wash.Wash cold, hang dry to minimize shrinkage. Or buy one size larger than you measured.
  5. Trust customer photos, not written reviews."100% of customers say true to size" is marketing. Real photos on real bodies tell the truth. Look for Nigerian buyers if possible.
  6. Nigerian bodies run curvier at hip and thighthan Asian sizing assumes. Especially for jeans and fitted trousers. Look for stretch fabric or size up.
